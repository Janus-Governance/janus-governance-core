## National assistance case

The emergency context in 2000–2001 must be distinguished from the creation and implementation of the Programa Jefes de Hogar in 2002. For the statement that the operational mandate concerned the program in 2001, read:

> Against the background of the 2000–2001 emergency, the Programa Jefes de Hogar was created in 2002 by Argentina's Decree 565/2002.

Decree 1369/2001, dated 29 October 2001 and published on 31 October 2001, is cited solely to establish the official denomination of the ministry in 2001. It does not document the emergency or create the program. The unsupported beneficiary and throughput figures, the specific PNUD attribution, and reference [3] are withdrawn. The author's participation and any uncited emergency context remain retrospective practitioner recollection.
