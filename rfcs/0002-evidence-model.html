RFC 0002 — Evidence Model (E+ / E−)

Status: Draft
Author: Martín Nicolás Sánchez Morales
Created: 2026
Aligned with: Core Lite v0.2
Canonical specification: core/lite/JANUS_CORE_LITE_v0.2.md

Authority and scope

Core Lite v0.2 is the primary normative authority. This Draft explains its evidence model and neither replaces nor extends it. If the texts differ, the canonical Core prevails. Frameworks, runtimes, demos, HTML, and suites do not redefine the Core.

Summary

E+ and E− are classes of evidence. They are not Boolean labels, event types, verdicts, or references. Governance events invoke evidence through resolvable references to immutable versions of supporting artifacts.

Specification

Explicit evidence (E+)

E+ is evidence explicitly recorded in a system of record. A claim supported by E+ requires an explicitly recorded artifact that can be retrieved and inspected. A governance event that invokes E+ references an immutable version of that artifact through a resolvable evidence reference.

Omission evidence (E−)

E− is evidence derived from a verified absence of an expected record or event. A simple absence, failed lookup, or missing value is not E−.

E− requires all of the following cumulative conditions:

  1. a defined, referenceable expectation whose version was recorded before the observation and declares provenance, temporal applicability, and when it becomes due;
  2. a deterministic scope identifying sources, subject, filters, window, cutoff (as-of), and the evaluation procedure with its version;
  3. a cutoff that does not precede the due time, with due status determinable from the material consulted up to that cutoff;
  4. the SCHEMA_LOG context applicable to every record or event considered up to the cutoff; and
  5. a reproducible observation relative to that scope and cutoff.

E− is not counter-argumentation. It is a verified omission relative to an explicit expectation.

Relationship with governance events

Evaluation may use E+ and/or E−. An instance of E− that satisfies the Core conditions may produce OMISSION_DETECTED. Neither E+ nor E− automatically produces a human decision.

HUMAN_DECISION represents a prior human act when explicit human accountability is required. Evaluation does not originate it, and evidence does not substitute for it.

Security considerations

Implementations must prevent mutable or unresolved evidence references, incomplete scopes, retroactively defined expectations, inconsistent schema contexts, and irreproducible observations from being treated as valid E−.
