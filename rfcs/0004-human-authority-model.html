RFC 0004 — Human Authority Model

Status: Draft
Author: Martín Nicolás Sánchez Morales
Created: 2026
Aligned with: Core Lite v0.2
Canonical specification: core/lite/JANUS_CORE_LITE_v0.2.md

Authority and scope

Core Lite v0.2 is the primary normative authority. This Draft explains the human-authority boundary. Identity resolution and evaluation of competence belong to the Framework; neither Framework nor this Draft may redefine Core.

Summary

HUMAN_DECISION is the only canonical human-authority event. It represents an explicit prior human act and is required when an outcome requires explicit human accountability. No agent, runtime, evaluation, or automated flow may originate it.

Specification

Human act and technical recording

Automated systems may analyze evidence, detect conditions, or recommend an action. Those stages are distinct from the accountable human act.

HUMAN_DECISION represents a prior human act by which a designated human authority made or confirmed a decision over identified objects. The event records that decision. No agent, runtime, evaluation, or automated flow may originate that human act. The Audit Writer may validate and incorporate the event but must not originate the human act. The technical writer is not the responsible human authority.

Event contract

A HUMAN_DECISION must include:

  • event_type and stable event_id;
  • actor_ref, identifying the responsible human authority;
  • scope_ref, declaring the competence invoked;
  • object_refs, a non-empty unordered set identifying exclusively the records or events decided;
  • occurred_at, the time of the human act; and
  • recorded_at, the time of incorporation into AUDIT_LOG.

It may include evidence_refs, management_refs, and schema_ref as evidence or context. None substitutes for object_refs. External artifacts and derived views are not admissible decision objects.

Each object_refs entry identifies its origin log and stable target identity, contains no duplicate target, resolves against history already incorporated in that log, and never self-references. The Audit Writer prevents incorporation if an object reference does not resolve. A constitutive decision references the previously recorded proposal, draft, or version that it constitutes.

Append-only effect

A human decision does not rewrite events, evidence, or domain records. Later interpretation, authorization, or declaration about a governance event is appended as a new HUMAN_DECISION. Corrections remain append-only in the canonical log to which they belong.

Security considerations

Implementations must prevent automated origin, unresolved decision objects, identity substitution, ambiguous scope, and direct writes that bypass the Audit Writer.
